# Dual-arm collision hulls

Conservative convex hulls of the real ReBeL link meshes, used **only** by the dual-arm model
(`urdf/rebel_arm.macro.xacro`, generated by `scripts/make_arm_macro.py` from `DUAL_COLLISION_OVERRIDES`).
The single-arm model (`igus_rebel_description`, the real robot) is unchanged and keeps its box/cylinder primitives
(except link5, which already uses `igus_rebel_description/meshes/rebel_d00617809/collision/link5_hull.stl`).

## Why

The single-arm primitives of link1-4 cover only a part of the real link: up to 85-88 mm of the real mesh surface lies
outside them (the box of link1/link3 covers only the 30 mm plate; the cylinder of link2/link4 misses the upper arm
end / forearm end); motor3 is 20 mm short on +y; motor1-5 and link6 are 6-7 mm short on the rims; base_link is 3 mm
short on its top face (audit: `scan_results/link5_collision_20261012/`). A proof that two arms moving at the same time
cannot collide needs collision geometry that contains the real link.

## How they are made (`scripts/make_collision_hulls.py`, deterministic)

1. Real mesh = the visual `.dae` with the visual origin of `igus_rebel2.urdf`.
2. L-shaped links (link1, link3, link4) and the C-shaped link2 are cut by axis-aligned planes (greedy, the plane that
   removes the most hull volume; a split must remove >= 6 % of the link's single-hull volume). The mesh is clipped
   by the plane (nothing is dropped), each side is hulled on its own, so every triangle of the real mesh lies in exactly
   one piece. link2's remaining splits gain < 3 % each and were not taken (the arm is tilted, axis planes do not fit it).
3. Each piece's exact hull is replaced by a low-poly circumscribed polytope (half-spaces over an icosphere, the
   largest hull-face normals, the axes and the cut-plane normals, each pushed out by 0.2 mm). Every real vertex satisfies
   every half-space by construction.
4. Verification (script exits non-zero otherwise, repeated from the written STL files and again in
   `test/test_dual_table_description.py`, which uses the installed meshes): all vertices and 400k (test: 100k) surface
   samples of the real mesh lie inside at least one piece of their link (qhull facet planes, tolerance 0.1 mm; actual
   margin 0.16-0.20 mm inside), every piece is a closed mesh with the volume of the convex hull of its vertices.

Regenerate with `python3 scripts/make_collision_hulls.py` (source tree only), check the installed STLs with
`--verify`, then `python3 scripts/make_arm_macro.py` and rebuild.

## Known remaining gaps

* link2 (3 pieces) and link1/link3 (2 pieces) still over-cover their hollow shells (volume 0.7-0.9 of the single hull);
  they are conservative, not tight.
* The tool links (`knife_c_*`) are not part of this table. The knife blade is visual only by design (see the single-arm
  notes); `knife_c_handpiece` collision leaves up to 14 mm of the blade outside.
