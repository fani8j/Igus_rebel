# gripper_tool - right-arm end effector (SMC LEHF20K2-48 in-line on the ReBeL flange, custom fingers)

Parametric, printable CAD for the RIGHT arm of the medical-box shelf-pick cell. The other arm's suction head pulls the thin carton 100 mm out of the shelf;
this gripper comes down from above, the two fingers straddle the 12-16 mm thickness, a fixed centre top-stop registers the box top edge, the pads clamp the two big faces
15-70 mm behind the spine. All coordinates are mm in the URDF **link6** frame (flange face z = 52.7), tool axis +z, stroke axis X, pad width along Y.
Shared inputs are imported read-only from `../common/params.py`; tool values are in `params.py`. No ROS node and no robot was used.

Rebuild: `./run_all.sh` (about 1 minute: `build.py` -> `verify.py` -> `render.py`; venv python of the shared params, scratch on the external drive).

## Analysis of the vendor STEP and the catalog
* STEP frame: x = stroke axis, y = thickness (-35..0), z = length: rear face z = -46, jaw end z = +37. Three solids: body + 2 separate jaw solids, so **the jaws are moved individually** (jaw opening o: pad gap = 8 + 2 o).
* The STEP is in the **fully closed** state (jaw inner faces touch at x = 0, catalog "closed 0"). At o = 24 the outer jaw end is at x = -53.0, 0.8 mm inside the body slot end (-53.8): consistent with the 24 mm per-jaw travel.
* **Body mount**: 4 x M5 (tap d4.13 x 7.6 deep; catalog M5x0.8x8) on the face z = -46, which is the face OPPOSITE the jaws (verified), pattern 40 x 22 (x = +-20, y = +-11 in link6); two d4H9 x 3 deep dowel holes at x = 0, y = +-11 (one round, one 1 mm slot). That face is x -52.3..60.7, y +-15.8 (not 158 x 35: the 158 includes the motor cable strain relief).
* **Jaw faces**: finger-mounting plane z_s = 37.0 (two raised 0.2 mm strips, y +-(4..10)); 4 x M3 (tap d2.46, ~3.8 deep) per jaw at x = -23.5, -5.5 (left jaw) / +5.5, +23.5 (right jaw), y = +-7.5 (catalog "8 x M3 at +-5.5 / +-23.5" = 4 per jaw, measured from the centre line at the closed position); 1 x d2.5H9 x 2.5 dowel hole per jaw at |x| = 23.5, y = 0; plus a d6 clearance hole at |x| = 13 (unused).
* **Catalog L and H** (p.43-45): L = distance from the finger mounting plane (guide) to the gripping point along the finger (here z); H = overhang of the gripping point from the guide centre line perpendicular to stroke and finger (here Y). 100 % force needs H + L <= 55, 70 % / 40 % <= 80. Per-finger force at 100 %: 28 N (L = 10) -> 23 N (L = 55); 70 %: 19.7 -> 14; 40 %: 11.3 -> 8; accuracy +-25 % F.S. Allowable moments (p.45): Mp (about Y) 0.68, My (about Z) 0.68, Mr (about X, stroke axis) 1.4 N m, Fv 98 N. The catalog also asks for a gripping force of 10-20 x the workpiece weight (14.7-29 N for 0.15 kg), which 100 % meets.

## Design
### Flange adapter: two parts (every screw reachable)
The gripper rear face covers 4 of the 6 PCD-43 M3 holes (those at |y| < 15.8: 45, 165, 225, 345 deg) and the M5 holes sit 5.5 mm from an M3 hole, so a one-piece plate cannot be assembled.
* `flange_disc` (R36 x 8.3): 6 x countersunk M3 on PCD 43 (primary, 45 + 60k deg) **and** 6 on PCD 35 (spare, 15 + 60k), countersinks on the gripper side, so the disc is screwed to the robot from the outside; 4 x heat-set M4 inserts (hole d5.6 x 4.5, floor 3.8) at (+-14, +-25.5) = outside the 35 mm gripper footprint; 2 dowel pockets d4.1 x 1.5. **Flange screws: M3x12 ISO 10642 = 12 - 8.3 = 3.7 mm engagement in the 4 mm flange threads (>= 3.5, 0.3 mm short of bottoming).**
* `gripper_plate` (58 x 62 x 6, clipped by R40): 4 x countersunk M5 from the flange side (heads flush, the plate sits flat on the disc), 4 x M4 clearance at the insert positions (heads at |y| >= 22, 4.6 mm clear of the gripper side), 2 x d4.05 dowel holes. Two ISO 8734 4x10 pins locate gripper (3 mm) - plate (6 mm) - disc (1 mm).
* Assembly order: (1) bench: pins into the gripper dowel holes, plate on the pins (gripper upside down), 4 x M5x12 countersunk from the plate underside into the gripper (6 mm engagement); heat-set the 4 M4 inserts into the disc and 2 M3 inserts into each finger. (2) Disc onto the robot, 6 x M3x12 countersunk from the +z side (nothing is in the way yet). (3) Gripper + plate onto the disc, 4 x M4x10 from +z (vertical hex key at |y| = 25.5, outside the gripper). (3b) Stop yoke: slide it down from +z over the body (side plates alongside the body faces, crossbar above the jaws; checked collision free for open and closed jaws), 2 x M5x55 through the body holes x = +-15 with nyloc nuts on the -y side (remove the yoke before touching the M4 screws). (4) With the jaws OPEN: dowel pin 2.5x6 into each jaw, finger on the jaw; the two outer M3x8 low-head screws go in vertically; the two inner screws are dropped through the 10.5 mm window under the pad block and driven with a ball-end 2 mm hex key swung >= 15 deg toward the gap (checked: collides at 10 deg, free from 15 deg). (5) Pads into the pockets, 2 x M3x5 button screws each into the finger inserts. Removal is the reverse; the motor cable leaves on the -X side (right-hand entry option / cable routing is the user's choice). **No dowel exists on the ReBeL flange: re-teach the TCP after mounting.**

### Fingers (left finger = x < 0, right finger = its mirror image)
Foot (jaw face, 7 mm, 4 counterbored M3 + dowel hole) -> thin 6 mm column over a 10.5 mm **window** (screw access) -> 12.6 mm thick pad block from z = 17.5 with the 45 deg x 5 mm lead-in chamfer at the tip.
The pad pocket (50.4 x 45.4 x 2.6, 3 mm walls) takes a **2.6 mm TPU insert with 0.4 mm horizontal ribs (0.6 wide, 1.5 pitch) = 3.0 mm total, crest at |x| = 4.0**; two M3x5 button screws (heads sunk 1.9 mm in the TPU) into M3 heat-set inserts retain it mechanically (no glue, replaceable, cleanable). Pad centre L = 43.2.
The box top edge registers on the **fixed centre top-stop** (below), not on a finger: the stop never moves with the jaws, so a descent with the fingers fully open is stopped by contact.
**Registration procedure (v3, position based: the ReBeL has no force sensing, nothing is ever pushed to a force or z stop).** Frame: tool z = link6 z; the box top edge is at tool-z distance `d_edge` beyond the stop face; stop face z = 167.5, jaw plane 150.0, fingertips 226.9.
1. **Plan z.** Box top edge world z SHOULD come from a vision measurement of the pulled carton when available (left-arm wrist camera before pressing, or the scene camera); fallback: shelf floor z (cabinet model / camera) + carton height h (GS1 barcode lookup). Final tool pose: stop face **G_NOM = 4.0 mm on the flange side of the top edge** (tool z of the stop face = z_edge - 4.0). Assumed stack, +- mm: height lookup 1.0, shelf floor 1.5, TCP z calibration 1.0, spine pitch 3 deg over the 27.5 mm half length 1.4: worst case +-4.9, RSS +-2.5.
2. **Align.** Tool axis tilted to the measured box lean (residual +-1 deg), pad axis X along the box thickness, lateral (X) error vs. the box centre <= 8 mm (calibration + 2 deg lean at 230 mm).
3. **Open pre-position.** Jaws fully open (56 mm) over the carton with the fingertips >= 10 mm above the top edge (tool z of the tips = z_edge - 10).
4. **Pre-close (stage a).** Close to a pad gap of **t + 4 mm** (t from the GS1 lookup, nominal) with the tips still above the edge; tip-to-tip gap is then t + 20.
5. **Descend (stage b)** along the tool axis, speed <= 20 mm/s, to the final pose of step 1 and **stop exactly at the computed pose: no force/z search, no creeping down until something happens**. The 8 mm 45 deg chamfers catch the top edge within **+-8.95 mm** (worst case t = 12...16 incl. thickness tolerance, backlash and print tolerance), centre it to <= 2.75 mm residual (2.0 nominal); the box passes 55.4 mm between the pads before the stop face arrives.
6. **Clamp (stage c)** at 100 % pushing force, pushing speed <= 20 mm/s (24.3 N per finger at L = 43.2). Read the jaw position back: grip ok if the pad gap is within t +- 1 mm and > 8.5 mm (a missed box closes to 8 mm).
7. **Release the suction** and lift. The stop face is now 4.0 mm (worst case -0.9 ... +8.9 mm, RSS 1.5 ... 6.5 mm) above the top edge; it does not carry load unless the box slips spine-up toward it (hold-torque job).
Check in `data/verify.json` -> `registration_v3`. If the stop face lands on the top edge (only if the height error exceeds 4.0 mm, about 5.6 % under the assumed stack) the arm pushes the carton down against the shelf (position controlled): see Mismatches 1b.

### Fixed centre top-stop (v3 section)
* Crossbar: plate in the YZ plane, centred on x = 0, **3 mm thick** (v2: 4 mm; 2.5 mm to each closed pad crest, 2.9 to the finger frame faces), y = -27.5...+27.5 (s = 15...70), z = 160.0...167.5 (3.0 mm above the finger feet); **face (toward the box) at z = 167.5** (frame `top_stop_frame`). It is no longer a landing reference: it only catches the box if it slips spine-up while hanging, reacting the hang torque at its spine-side end (s = 15, directly over a riser).
* Route outside the finger sweep: risers x +-1.5, |y| = 22.5...27.5 (feet end at |y| = 20: 2.5 mm) run down to two side plates (|x| <= 19, |y| = 17.5...24, z = 104...147.5; 2.5 mm below the feet) with ears (|x| <= 6, |y| to 27.5) so the riser sits fully on the plate; clamped on the SMC body side faces by 2 x M5x55 + nyloc through the existing d5.2 body holes (x = +-15, z = 113). No new holes; independent of the jaws.
* **Clearance option chosen (v3):** thinner crossbar (3 mm PA12-CF) + riser moved out to |y| >= 22.5 + crossbar raised 1.5 mm + side plates lowered to 2.5 mm below the feet; no finger change. Measured minimum distance stop -> fingers/pads/jaws: 6.5 mm open, **2.5 mm in all closed states (full close with no box, closed on 16 / 12, mid)**; boolean interference 0.0 mm3 in all 5 states.
* **Stiffness** under 15 N toward the body at the spine-side end: voxel FE (1 mm hex8, PA12-CF E = 3.2 GPa, bolt clamp zones fixed) gives 0.010 mm = **1450 N/mm** (v2: 1250); bolt/hole clearance (about 0.2 mm on the diameter) and clamp friction are not included. A 3 mm aluminium blade is not needed.
* Hold check (a): the pad friction alone must hold the box until it has slipped spine-up by the gap G (rotation G / 27.5 mm = 8.3 deg at 4.0 mm, 18.6 deg at the worst-case 8.9 mm); the numbers 'with stop' apply after that slip (`hold_check_a_with_stop` -> `stop_engaged_after_slip`), the 'no stop' numbers are the no-slip capacity. Rotate-to-hang tilt (zero torque) angles are unchanged: 42.7 / 41.2 / 44.4 / 46.6 / 42.7 deg for boxes 1-5.

## Mismatches / assumptions / unconfirmed
1. **Hold margin is still modest for box 4 at 11 N (no slip 0.64, after slip 0.99) and 18.2 N (1.06 / 1.64)**: use pushing force 100 % (24.3 N: 1.41 / 2.18). Static lower-bound model, mu 0.5, CoG at the box centre, 0.15 kg assumed. Use the rotate-to-hang attitude for the best margin.
1b. **Landing risk of the stop (v3.1, G_NOM = 4.0 mm, operator decision):** with the assumed +-4.9 mm worst-case (RSS 2.5) height stack the gap is -0.9 ... +8.9 mm worst case and +1.5 ... +6.5 mm RSS; landing probability about 5.6 % if the RSS is a 1 sigma Gaussian (needs G >= 4.9 for zero risk in the worst case, 5.0 for 2 sigma). A landing is not detected (no force sensing). Use the vision measurement of the top edge when available (it removes the carton-height and shelf-floor terms and cuts the stack to about +-2.4 mm worst case (TCP z 1.0 + pitch 1.4; the vision error itself is not included)) and treat the GS1 lookup as fallback. Cost of the larger gap: the box must slip 8.3 deg (18.6 deg worst case) before the stop engages; hold margins after the slip are in the table. The stack components are ASSUMED.
2. Bounding jaw-moment case (3 g along the stroke axis X, Mp) is 0.95 of the allowable (unchanged). The realistic case (tool pointing down, 2 g) passes with x1.42.
3. URDF finger velocity is now 0.05 m/s (catalog per-finger limit); pushing speed 5-30 mm/s.
4. M3 disc screws: M3x12 with the 8.3 mm disc = 3.7 mm engagement in the 4 mm threads; the thread depth (about 4 mm) and the PCD 35 pattern (10 mm?) are unverified on the robot (no dowel, no ISO 9409 pilot).
5. Stack-up is 14.3 mm (disc 8.3, earlier 7.5 / 13.5; the earlier tooling used 6.5): jaw plane z = 150.0, TCP 193.2, stop face 167.5; the TPU under the retention screw head is only 0.7 mm (by design, compressible).
6. Printed masses are estimates (1.6 mm shell + 25 % infill; TPU solid); gripper mass 0.75 kg from the catalog distributed by volume (body 0.729, jaws 2 x 0.0104). Heat-set inserts are modelled as empty holes (point masses only in the mass table). Screws/pins are nominal cylinders (no threads).
7. Finger stiffness is a first-order beam model (rigid jaw interface, TPU pad ignored, isotropic E 3.2 GPa PA12-CF / 1.8 GPa PETG): jaw backlash (0.5 mm per side, catalog) will dominate any finger deflection.
8. Not analysed: shelf lean (up to 10 deg) beyond the +-1 deg tool-tilt compensation assumed in step 2; carton crush strength (100 kPa assumed); the suction head volume itself (only the stated s margins); cable routing of the LEHF motor cable; pad wear/cleaning; the stop sees only the top edge region s = 15...70 (a box pitched 3 deg touches at one end).
9. Visual mesh of the vendor body is a raw tessellation (not watertight); collision meshes are convex hulls (body in 3 x-slabs, finger block hull ignores the pad pocket, jaws 2 hulls each). The URDF masses/inertias are from CAD solids with the estimated masses above.
10. SMC order-code suffixes (cable, motor-cable entry), the LEC/JXC driver choice and its I/O cable are marked 'to confirm' in the BOM; the LEHF always needs a driver (24 VDC +-10 %).
11. The Y axis of link6 is assumed to point along the box length (either sign works: the tool is symmetric about y = 0).
12. The root filesystem was at 0 bytes free for a moment during the first build (other sessions write there too); everything here is regenerated by `run_all.sh`.

## v2 -> v3
