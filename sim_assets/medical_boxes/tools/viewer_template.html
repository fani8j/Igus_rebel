Medical Carton Models

Single-unit catheter and stent cartons, built as USD assets for Isaac Sim. Everything on this page is read back from the generated .usda files: geometry, face textures, mass and verifier results.

Show Look at
Drag to orbit, scroll or pinch to zoom. Floor grid is 10 mm. Single cartons lie front-up; in the cabinet they stand spine-out.

Where the numbers come from

Dimensions are the caliper measurements (thickness × height × length). No manufacturer, regulator database (AccessGUDID, EUDAMED, TFDA, NHS Supply Chain) or distributor we could reach publishes unit-carton dimensions or weights. Straight-on reseller photos were used as an independent check of each face's proportions where one exists.

Mass is an estimate: 350 g/m² folding board × 1.25 for flaps × outer area, plus 50 g of contents. Replace it in boxes.yaml with a scale reading.

    Open in Isaac Sim

    File › Open sim_assets/medical_boxes/usd/medical_boxes_scene.usda and press Play. To use a single carton, add a reference to usd/<box>.usda; keep textures/ next to usd/.

    The labelled spine is the +Y face. For a shelf with cartons standing like books, rotate so +Y points out of the shelf and X is vertical.
